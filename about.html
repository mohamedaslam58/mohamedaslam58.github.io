About the Founder

Rooted in Modern Architecture & Timeless Elegance

Mohamed Azarudeen is the Founder and Project Architect of Azarch Design Studio, an architectural and interior design firm based in India, with 7 years of professional experience in architecture and design. He completed his Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch) in 2019 and is a Registered Architect with the Council of Architecture (COA), India.

Currently working as a Project Architect at an Engineering Consultancy Firm in Dubai, UAE, Mohamed has developed professional expertise in architectural design, project coordination, and luxury residential villa development across Dubai and India. His experience includes designing luxury villas, developing contemporary architectural concepts, and coordinating design solutions that combine aesthetics, functionality, and premium materials.

Through Azarch Design Studio, Mohamed aims to create distinctive architectural and interior spaces that reflect each client's vision, lifestyle, and aspirations. His design philosophy is rooted in modern architecture, thoughtful planning, and timeless elegance, delivering spaces where creativity meets functionality.
